# Added

Optional Fatal Strikes with three colored break gauges, timed openings and
chains, remappable controls, companion support, boss-safe damage and reward
bonuses. All nine characters use their own rigs for staged finishers with
cinematic cameras, weapon trails and original geometric effects. Enable the
system in Customize; it is off by default.

# Changed

Genis and Raine use ranged Fatal Strike performances with distinct casting-hand
arcs, constrained foot plants, and spell geometry attached to the animated palm.
Their three colors erupt upward, strike from above, or project toward the enemy.
Caster impacts preserve damage and defeat feedback while replacing ordinary hit
and death bursts with the spell's own effects. Genis channels with empty hands
and restores his equipped weapon on completion or cancellation. Bounded camera
curves prevent the close-up from overshooting through a character's face.

The other seven fighters use continuous baked performances with separate
anticipation, strike and follow-through paths for their fighting styles and
formula colors. Admission places the measured equipment contact on the enemy;
the body release and lethal impact share one clock. Colette throws two spinning
chakrams along color-specific paths and catches them during recovery. Regal
strikes with a leg while retaining his chained-hand guard. Shield users brace
their off arm; Presea compresses into a heavier wind-up and impact.
Weapon bounds over the complete performance determine action framing. Grounded
strikes keep the target's ground plane, and aerial executions land during
recovery before the idle handoff. Contact effects use the authored strike height.

Physical strikes use depth-tested world geometry for cuts, shock rings,
sparks and Sheena's talisman seal. These replace screen-space impact strokes
and ordinary hit/death bursts while retaining native hit sounds and enemy voices.
The showcase recorder tracks choreography revisions and its assembly tool checks
the opening, lethal contact, disappearance, frame count and synchronized PCM
for every recorded variation.
