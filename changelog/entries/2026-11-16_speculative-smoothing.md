# Added

Optional smoothing above 60 FPS through Customize's **Enable experimental high FPS** setting or `--speculative`, interpolating cameras, animation, effects and UI toward an isolated prediction of the next simulation tick. Prediction work is spread across frames at every frame rate. F10 cycles through half, fifth and tenth speed for inspecting motion, with audio following the selected speed.
