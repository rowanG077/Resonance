About Resonance

Resonance is a reimplementation of Tales of Symphonia, written in Rust with Bevy.

The aim is to preserve the original gameplay, visuals and audio on modern computers, with editable game assets and support for mods.

Before you play

Current releases require your own images of both North American GameCube discs, GQSEAF revision 0.

Use the importer to prepare the game assets, then start Resonance.
